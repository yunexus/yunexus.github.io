This paper presents SecuAudit, a decentralized auditing scheme for MCP-enabled AI agents that cryptographically binds metadata constraints, including access permissions and validity periods, to data authentication. By distributing key management across multiple servers through threshold secret sharing, the scheme reduces reliance on centralized trust while enabling privacy-preserving verification of data integrity and metadata compliance.
