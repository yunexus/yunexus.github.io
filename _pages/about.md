I am a second-year M.Sc. student in Artificial Intelligence at the School of Computer and Artificial Intelligence, Zhengzhou University. I am advised by Prof. Zhijuan Jia (Second-Level Professor) and expect to graduate in 2028.

My research focuses on trustworthy medical AI, with broader interests in deep learning, machine learning, computer vision, and applied cryptography. I am particularly interested in building dependable intelligent systems for real-world settings.

Outside research, I enjoy sharing technical insights on my CSDN blog, where I have gained over 400 followers. I also love exploring the outdoors and embracing new challenges with curiosity and optimism.
